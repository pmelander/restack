# Registers - surface form guidance

`register` is the dimension most often left out of a taxonomy like this and the
most worth keeping. Without it every statement reads like a newswire, which is
itself a form of collapse even when the content varies - and a workshop reading
thirty identical-sounding lines stops reading them.

Hand the renderer the register's character, not a template. A template produces
thirty statements with the same skeleton and different nouns.

## wire copy

Flat, attributed, no adjectives. Leads with what happened, not why. Tends to
carry a number and a place.

> The regional water authority extended abstraction limits to four additional
> municipalities on Tuesday, citing reservoir levels.

## official statement

Institutional voice, passive where it protects, dates and process nouns. Says
less than it appears to.

> Following a review, the Authority has determined that current arrangements
> will be suspended pending the outcome of consultation.

## social post

Fragmentary, present tense, first-hand, unattributed. Often the first signal
and often partly wrong - which is exactly what makes it a stressor worth
walking, since a system receives it before anyone has confirmed it.

> third day with no deliveries at the depot. nobody's saying anything

## overheard claim

Second-hand, hedged by the speaker rather than the source, specific about the
wrong things. What a team actually gets first, in practice.

> Someone from the ministry told our operations lead they are pulling the
> licence next quarter, though he had heard it as this quarter.

## analyst note

Forward-looking, comparative, quantified, confident beyond the evidence. Cites
its own prior note.

> We expect enforcement to follow the pattern of the 2019 restrictions, with
> compliance costs concentrated in the first two quarters.

## What not to do

Do not let the register carry the plausibility. An `absurd` statement in wire
copy register is still wire copy - flat, attributed, unremarkable in tone.
Reporting an impossible event in a breathless voice tells the reader it is the
one to dismiss, which is precisely the reaction the blind track exists to
prevent.
